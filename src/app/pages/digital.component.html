FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS / 07

Before we begin. A few answers.

Some useful information to help you understand how we approach digital projects.

01What kind of digital projects do you develop?We work on business websites, web applications, ERP platforms, booking systems, business portals, UI/UX design and custom digital solutions.+
02Is RANGRIOT.UNIFIED ERP your own platform?Yes. RANGRIOT.UNIFIED ERP is our own enterprise business management platform, developed to connect business operations through one system.+
03Can you build a solution around my business requirements?Yes. We first understand your business processes and define a suitable solution based on the requirements and agreed project scope.+
04How much does a website or application cost?Pricing depends on project complexity, features, integrations, design requirements and delivery scope. We provide a proposal after understanding the project.+
05How long does a project take?Timelines depend on the scope, functionality, integrations and approval process. An estimated schedule is shared during project planning.+
06Do you provide maintenance and support?Maintenance and technical support can be included through an agreed support arrangement based on the project's requirements.+
